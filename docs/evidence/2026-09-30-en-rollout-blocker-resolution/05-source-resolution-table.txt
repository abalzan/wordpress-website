THE SEVEN NO_REAL_PT_SOURCE ROWS — AN EDITORIAL DECISION, NOT A SILENT EXCLUSION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The default behaviour required by the task is "do not delete the authored
translation merely to make the gate green". That default was followed exactly:

* No authored EN row was deleted. All 7 remain, with their full authored
  `en_slug`, `en_title`, `en_excerpt`, `en_meta_description` and `en_content`
  intact and byte-unchanged in the repository datasets.
* No row was silently converted into an exclusion. No allowlist, skip-list or
  "ignore" entry was added anywhere.
* No placeholder PT content was created, in the repository or in production.
* No PT record was created, renamed, merged or deleted to make a row resolve.
* No PT content was modified. The PT identity digest is unchanged (see
  `01-en-inventory-manifest.json` -> `report.pt_baseline`).

They are classified EXPLICITLY, and the engine's own vocabulary is used, so the
classification is visible in the plan rather than hidden in a comment. The
repository's shared engine already has exactly the right disposition for a row
with no PT record — `build_plan()` emits
`'PT record absent in this site (documented exclusion)'` into the `skip` bucket
— so these 7 rows are planned as documented skips and are reported by name in
`04-summary-tables.txt` under "missing PT sources (MISSING_SOURCE)".

THE EDITORIAL DECISION EACH ROW STILL OWES
------------------------------------------
Exactly one of the following, decided by an editor and not by this phase:

  (a) RETIRE the authored EN row, if the PT article it translates no longer
      exists in the editorial plan;
  (b) EXCLUDE it explicitly, with a documented reason, if the article is being
      re-published under a new PT identity that has not been created yet; or
  (c) RE-KEY it later, once the real PT source exists — which is a separate
      content-creation task and explicitly out of scope here.
