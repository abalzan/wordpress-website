SEARCH METHODOLOGY — EN PT SOURCE RECONCILIATION
================================================

ORDER OF SEARCH (repository first, then production, as the task requires)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. REPOSITORY — the authored EN rows themselves, at HEAD, read out of the
   plugin's own data files by executing them in PHP (no reimplementation).
2. REPOSITORY — historical PT identity: the committed Stage-M capture and the
   committed EN-Guides PT snapshot. These record the REAL PT ids these rows were
   translated from (Level 1 evidence).
3. PRODUCTION (read-only, GET) — a fresh full read of every published PT record
   in the three B1 post types: posts, pages, guide (136 records).
4. PRODUCTION — exact slug match, then exact historical id match, then a
   distinctive-entity search, then a token/similarity ranking of every live
   candidate against the authored EN body.
5. PRODUCTION — a direct per-id probe across all EIGHT post types with
   status=any, so a record that survived as a draft, a private post or under a
   different post type would still be found.

MATCHING HIERARCHY ACTUALLY APPLIED
-----------------------------------
Level 1 exact historical identity  : applied first. It identified all seven
                                     historical PT sources by real id.
Level 2 known slug migration       : applied. No successor slug is recorded in
                                     the repository for any of the seven, and
                                     none exists in production.
Level 3 strong-content match       : applied exhaustively to the two rows whose
                                     authored EN body carries real content
                                     (rows 1 and 4) and to row 5 (a byline
                                     stub). Provably IMPOSSIBLE for rows 2, 3, 6
                                     and 7, because both the historical PT body
                                     and the authored EN body are the empty
                                     placeholder '<p></p>'. An empty body
                                     cannot be content-matched: there is no
                                     content on either side. This is stated as a
                                     fact, not treated as a weak match.

WHY CONTENT MATCHING IS EXHAUSTIVE HERE
----------------------------------------
Every live PT record was scored against the authored EN body with BOTH a token
Jaccard index over >=4-character tokens and a difflib SequenceMatcher ratio over
the normalised text, and the full ranking was inspected. A genuine translation
pair scores far above noise; the best false-positive scores measured 0.08
(Jaccard) and 0.017 (sequence) for row 4, and 0.043 / 0.006 for row 1. Those are
the numbers that make the negative result a measurement rather than an
assumption.

IDENTIFIERS
-----------
Production ids are used ONLY as verification evidence, exactly as the task
requires. They are never proposed as cross-environment authored identity. The
authored identity the repository supports remains the PT stable key (slug).
Where a row's stable key is no longer resolvable, that is reported as a finding
for the operator, not papered over with a production id.
