NOTE ON THE TRASH FINDING — WHY NOTHING WAS RESTORED
---------------------------------------------------
Six of the seven PT sources were found in production TRASH. Restoring them would
be a production CONTENT WRITE, which this phase is explicitly forbidden from
doing ("Production must remain completely read-only", "No production content
mutation"). The task's outcome vocabulary only offers RE-KEY (a repository
change), REQUIRES HUMAN REVIEW and BLOCKED — it offers no "restore the PT record"
action, and a restore is an editorial/content decision that belongs to the
operator, not to a source-reconciliation investigation.

So the trashed records are reported as EVIDENCE of identity, not acted upon. The
mapping below therefore records the true PT source for each row and states, per
row, that un-trashing is a prerequisite the operator must authorise before a
re-key can resolve in production.
