## 7. The anonymous surface is CDN-cached — a finding worth recording

A later anonymous re-read at `2026-11-05T15:53:50Z` (no cache-buster) returned
`modified: 2026-08-21T21:48:44`, i.e. a **stale cached copy** from before the test.
It is caching, not a rollback:

| Probe | `modified` | lat / lng |
|---|---|---|
| anonymous, no cache-buster (15:53:50Z) | `2026-08-21T21:48:44` (**stale**) | `""` / `""` |
| anonymous, cache-busted (`&cb=<epoch>`) | `2026-11-05T15:46:00` | `""` / `""` |
| **authenticated `context=edit`** | `2026-11-05T15:46:00` (`modified_gmt` identical) | `""` / `""` |

Response header on the identity probe: `x-ac: 19.gru _atomic_dfw MISS` — WordPress.com
Atomic's CDN. **An anonymous REST read must be cache-busted (or use `context=edit`)
before being treated as current.** The metadata values agreed in all three probes, so no
safety conclusion depends on the cached copy; only the `modified` stamp did. Recorded so
a future verifier who sees the old timestamp does not mistake it for a rollback.
