G18 — mutation negative controls
=====================================================================

Command
-------
    bash tests/experiments/g18-mutations.sh [M…]

Every mutation breaks EXACTLY ONE mechanism and REQUIRES
`test-import-continuation.php` to fail. A mutation that leaves the suite green is
reported as `VACUOUS` and fails the script, because a green suite under a
mutation proves the assertion was not protecting anything.

The exit code is the authoritative signal; the "N passed, M failed" line is the
human note. `assertions.php` exits 1 immediately on a failed prerequisite, so a
genuine failure can print no summary line at all — that case is reported as
"the suite exited early on a failed assertion", never as a pass. A mutant that
merely hangs is reported separately as TIMEOUT, and is never presented as an
assertion-level proof.

HOW THIS WAS RECORDED, HONESTLY
-------------------------------
M1–M14 were produced by one full run of the script. M6 came back VACUOUS in that
run (256 passed, 0 failed), for a real reason: in every REACHABLE state the
checkpoint's `complete` flag and its page-count comparison agree, so removing
one of the two terms changed no reachable answer. That made the term
undetectable AND made its original docblock justification inaccurate.

Two changes followed rather than deleting the term or weakening the mutation:
  1. the docblock now states what the term means (a finished generation is
     finished), with the inaccurate claim removed; and
  2. the suite gained a predicate-precision block that asserts the flag
     directly, including the one case that distinguishes it — a `complete`
     record is NOT resumable even when a later listing declares more pages,
     while the same record with `complete = false` IS.

M2 was also redefined after the first run: it originally skipped the
arming-FAILURE branch, which is now M15. M2 now removes the arming itself
(`arm_cron` returns true without scheduling), which is the mutation the label
describes.

The three affected mutations were then re-run on the corrected script and the
strengthened suite:

    M2   the trigger is never armed              PROVEN  (the suite exited early on a failed assertion)
    M6   the complete term is ignored            PROVEN  (263 passed, 1 failed)
    M15  the arming-failure branch is skipped    PROVEN  (the suite exited early on a failed assertion)

    PROVEN (suite failed as required): 3
    VACUOUS or broken:                0

The vacuous M6 result is kept in this record rather than overwritten: it is the
evidence that the controls were doing their job.

SAFETY
------
The script mutates only the local working tree and always restores the
originals from a backup (trap on EXIT/INT/TERM); every mutant is
`php -l`-checked so a failure is behavioural and never a parse error. It never
contacts a production host and never writes production.
